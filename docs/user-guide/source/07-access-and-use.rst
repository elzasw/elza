Conditions of access and use
============================

Access
------

ISAD(G) 3.4.1 records the conditions governing access as a statement. The
rule set keeps that statement and adds two elements a system can act on:

- *Conditions governing access* - the statement: the law, the agreement
  with the donor, the state of processing that restricts access, and what
  the reader must do to obtain it.
- *Access status* - **open**, **restricted** or **closed**. A unit without
  the element is treated as open. The status of a unit applies to the
  units below it unless they carry their own.
- *Open from* - the date on which a restricted or closed unit becomes
  open, when it is known (the end of a closure period, the death of the
  person concerned).

Reproduction (3.4.2) stays a statement in *Conditions governing
reproduction*: copyright, the conditions of copying and of publication.

Version 1 of the rule set does not model the reasons of restriction, the
events that lift them or the publication of restricted units; these
depend on the legislation of the country and belong to an addon package
of the installation (implementation guide). The three elements above are
what an addon and the outputs build on.

Language and script
-------------------

*Language of material* (3.4.3) is chosen from the languages of ISO 639-2 -
the same list the entities use for the languages of their names - and is
repeatable. The languages used most are offered first (English, French,
German, Spanish, Latin); the rest are found by typing their name.
*Script of material* is a text, e.g. "Latin", "Cyrillic", "Kurrent".

Physical characteristics
------------------------

*Physical characteristics and technical requirements* (3.4.4) describes
the condition of the material and what is needed to use it: fragile
paper, a magnetic tape that needs a player, a file format that needs a
viewer.

Finding aids
------------

*Finding aids* (3.4.5) names the finding aids that exist for the unit,
printed or electronic, including those of the same fonds in another
repository. The finding aid ELZA generates from the description itself is
described in :doc:`09-outputs`.

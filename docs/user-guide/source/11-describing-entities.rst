Entities: describing an entity
==============================

.. todo::

   Written in a later step, with the package ``ISAAR_CPF``. Its scope, by
   the areas of ISAAR(CPF):

   - **Identity area** - the authorized form of the name and its other
     forms (parallel, translated, former, pseudonym, acronym ...), the
     language and the dates of use of each form; the identifiers of the
     entity in other systems (VIAF, ISNI, GND, Wikidata, ORCID, GeoNames
     ...) with their validity;
   - **Description area** - the dates and kinds of the beginning and the
     end of existence; the history, the abstract, the general context; for
     a corporate body its type, legal status, mandates, internal
     structure, functions and places; for a family its type and genealogy;
     for a place its type, coordinates and the place it is part of; for a
     concept the scheme it belongs to;
   - **Events** - dated facts of the existence of the entity (an
     occupation, a membership, a study, an award, a change of legal
     status) with the entities they relate to;
   - what the rules require (at least one name, one description, one
     beginning and one end of existence) and the validation messages;
   - the index names: how the name of a person, a body or a place is
     shown and sorted.
